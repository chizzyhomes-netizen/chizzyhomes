About ChizzyHomes

Built specifically for FUT Minna students.

ChizzyHomes is a dedicated prop-tech platform engineered to solve the stress, uncertainty, and agent scams associated with securing student accommodation around Gidan Kwano and Bosso campuses.

Our mission is simple: provide 100% verified lodge details, clear utility specs (Starlink, water, transformers), and direct pathways to reliable landlords.

01. Why We Started

Finding a safe and comfortable lodge as a student shouldn't feel like navigating a minefield of fake fees and misleading pictures. ChizzyHomes bridges the gap by centralizing trusted housing options with transparent parameters.

02. What We Offer

  • Verified Listings: Properties reviewed to match real-world specifications.
  • Interactive Mapping: Real-time location plotting to gauge proximity to lecture halls and gates.
  • Direct Access: Seamless connection with verified caretakers without exploitation.
