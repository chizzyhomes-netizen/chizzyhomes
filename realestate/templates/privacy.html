Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

At ChizzyHomes, protecting your personal data is a top priority as we help students find secure accommodation around FUT Minna.

🛡️ We only collect information essential for delivering verified lodge listings and maintaining secure user accounts. We never sell your personal details.

01. Information We Collect

To provide a seamless experience on our platform, we may gather:

  • Account registration credentials (username, email address, secure passwords).
  • Property listing and agent interaction records if you manage or bookmark lodges.
  • Basic technical logs and local storage preferences (such as recently viewed lodges).

02. How We Use Your Data

The information collected is used strictly to:

  • Authenticate user login sessions and dashboard privileges.
  • Connect property seekers directly with verified student lodge providers.
  • Ensure platform security, prevent fraudulent entries, and update listing features.

03. Data Security Standards

We apply robust administrative and technical security protocols to safeguard your information from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

04. Policy Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect platform upgrades. Continued use of ChizzyHomes constitutes your agreement to these practices.

Questions about privacy? Reach out to us.
